What is German IPTV and what do you get?

IPTV simply means television delivered over the internet instead of cable, satellite or an aerial. For people who have moved to Germany, Austria or Switzerland, it solves a common problem: you want the local German channels to follow the news and improve your German, but you also want TV from home. A German IPTV service combines both in one channel list. With our service, every plan includes the same content:

  • More than 50,000 live channels, including German free-TV channels such as ARD, ZDF, RTL and ProSieben, news, documentary, children's, music, film and sports channels, and international channels from many countries.
  • More than 300,000 films and series as video on demand (VOD), which you start whenever you like.
  • Full HD and 4K where the original source broadcasts in that quality.
  • EPG (electronic programme guide) so you can see what is on now and next, and catch-up for many channels so you can rewatch recent programmes.
  • Support in German via WhatsApp, including help with setting up your app.

The service also works with a VPN switched on, which is useful if you travel or prefer to keep your connection private. If you want to understand the technology in more depth, our basics article Was ist IPTV? (German guide) explains how streams, playlists and players fit together.

Setup in three steps

  1. Get your login. Request the free test or place an order. You receive an M3U link, an Xtream Codes login (server URL, username, password) and, if you use a MAG or similar set-top box, a portal URL.
  2. Install a player app. Download TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, IBO Player Pro or another player from your device's official app store. Avoid modified versions from unknown websites.
  3. Enter the data and wait. Choose “Xtream Codes” as the login type where offered, as channel groups, EPG and the film library load automatically. The first load of a large list can take a minute or two.

Once the list has loaded, hide country groups you do not need so you can find German and home-country channels quickly. Our German guide IPTV einrichten shows where to enter the login in every common app, and the channel list guide explains how groups, favourites and the EPG are organised.

What internet speed do you need?

Most German home connections are fast enough. As a guide, plan about 10 Mbit/s for an HD stream and about 25 Mbit/s for a 4K stream, per stream running at the same time. Two people watching HD in different rooms therefore need around 20 Mbit/s of reliable speed. A LAN cable is more stable than Wi-Fi, especially for TVs and sticks placed far from the router; if a cable is not possible, a Wi-Fi mesh or repeater near the TV often helps.

In student halls and shared flats, the connection is often shared with many people and drops in the evening. Test the service at around 8 pm, when German prime time starts, before you commit to a longer plan. Mobile data works too, but a single HD film can use several gigabytes.

Can I watch German TV channels in English?

The German channels broadcast in German, and that does not change with IPTV. Whether a second audio track or subtitles are available depends on the broadcaster and the stream, so do not count on it. In addition, the list includes English-language channels from other countries, and many films and series in the VOD library are available in their original language.

Does German IPTV work with a VPN?

Yes, the service works with a VPN switched on. A VPN can add a little delay or reduce speed, so choose a server close to you. If a stream starts buffering only with the VPN active, try a different VPN server before contacting support.
